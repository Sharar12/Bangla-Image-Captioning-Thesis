# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What GPU is required to train BanglaVision?
Training the full SigLIP2 + SCST pipeline requires an NVIDIA GPU with at least 8 GB VRAM (RTX 3060, 4060, 5070 Ti, or A100).

### Can I run inference on CPU?
Yes! With our dynamic INT8 quantization, inference takes ~48 ms per image on standard modern CPUs.
